Privacy Policy — Family Hub

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Family Hub is a personal, non-commercial project built for private use by one family (the Bentons). It is not a public product or service, is not sold or offered to anyone outside this family, and has no commercial purpose.

What it accesses

Family Hub connects to a small number of Google services, solely to display information on a private wall-mounted dashboard inside our own home:

  • Google Calendar — read-only access to show upcoming family events.
  • Google Smart Device Management (Nest) — access to read and adjust our own home thermostat.

How data is used

Any data retrieved from these services is used only to render tiles on this private dashboard, and only for the family members who use it. Nothing is sold, shared, or made available to any third party, advertiser, or other service.

Where data is stored

Data is fetched on demand from Google's own APIs and is not copied into any separate database. No data is stored outside of Google's systems and the browser displaying the dashboard.
